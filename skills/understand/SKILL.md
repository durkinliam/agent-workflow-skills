# Understand

Establish the intended outcome from the prompt, code and documents. Trace affected
paths and consumers; test suspected causes against plausible alternatives.
Separate observations, hypotheses and proposed changes. Do not edit production.

Investigate technical unknowns. Ask only about intent or trade-offs evidence
cannot settle, starting with the question that unlocks the next decision.
Defer later questions with a resolution trigger. Agree any necessary experiment's
scope and distinguishing result before running it.

Produce a concise brief: outcome and constraints, current behaviour and source
references, findings and evidence, unknowns and next decisions. Make consequential
assumptions visible before they shape the plan.

Small investigations can finish in chat. After substantial exploration, preserve
the brief for planning, leaving search logs and abandoned hypotheses behind.
Stop when evidence supports the next decision; use Plan when authorized.
